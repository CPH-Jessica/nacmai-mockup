Hall of Fame Inductees, 1998–2026

The North American Country Music Associations, International (NACMAI) was founded by Mrs. Peggy Franck in 1997. Since the association's inception, over 130 Music Professionals have been inducted into the NACMAI Hall of Fame. NACMAI is a 501c3 non-profit Corp. operating under the laws of the US Government and the State of Tennessee. The NACMAI Hall of Fame is in place to honor and pay homage to the true legends of Country Music, as well as to keep Country Music close to its roots. Mrs. Peggy Franck, CEO & Founder was quoted saying, “Without the past, there is no future.” NACMAI’s goal is to instill and preserve the history and sounds of Country, Gospel, and Bluegrass music in the ears, minds, and hearts of today’s generation.

Every man and woman on this page earned their place by a lifetime of service to country, gospel, and bluegrass music. Names link to the artist's current website where one is available.
