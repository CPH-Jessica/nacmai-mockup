Welcome

In March of 1997, a small group of people who loved country music sat around a table and asked a simple question: where do the independent artists go? Nashville had its machine. The big labels had their pipeline. But what about the singer who could bring a room to its feet on a Saturday night, the gospel quartet, the bluegrass picker who never got a shot at a stage bigger than the county fair?

NACMAI was the answer. Today we are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit with state and international chapters across the United States and Canada, all sending their finest talent to the annual Country Music Week in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee — a week of showcases, competitions, and educational seminars that concludes with the Hall of Fame Show and the NACMAI Awards.

Our Mission

To promote the development of the Country, Bluegrass, and Gospel Music industry in each state and country through an international association — promoting, encouraging, and educating independent artists and fans, and bringing together the various state and international country music associations in an organized method.

“Keeping country country.”

That wasn't just a slogan. It was a promise — and every artist who has walked across our stage keeps that promise alive.

— Peggy Franck, Founder
