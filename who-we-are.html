Who We Are

We're not just an organization. We're a family — artists, parents, grandparents, state presidents, judges, and fans who show up every year and cheer like it matters. Because it does.

Country Music Week

Every spring, competitors representing chapters across the United States and Canada travel to Pigeon Forge, Tennessee for an exciting week of musical competition.

The week features musicians and artists performing New Country, Traditional Country, Bluegrass, Traditional Gospel, and New Gospel — vocalists, entertainers, instrumentalists, songwriters, duos, groups, and bands, in age divisions from 3–6 all the way to Adult 50+. Judging is done by industry professionals on quality of performance, originality, star quality, tightness, stage presence, and faithfulness to the style.

The competition concludes with the Hall of Fame Show on Saturday evening and the Awards Presentation on Sunday — two nights that celebrate both the legends who built this music and the artists carrying it forward.
